# "Mosquito: An Overreaction" — Director's Proposal + Prompt Package

9:16 vertical · 1080×1920 · 25 s · 30 fps · **Style 4 (Cozy Storytelling / 治愈故事风)**
English VO (deadpan documentary narrator, en-GB, masculine) with SFX and a comedy score.

The completed animation is `out/mosquito-vs-stickman.mp4`, rendered by this folder's
Python pipeline. The block below is the same film as a **Gemini Omni Flash prompt
package**, so it can be regenerated in Flow as three standalone ~10 s clips.

---

## The frozen voice profile (repeat verbatim in every clip)

> Dry British male documentary narrator, mid-forties, low-mid baritone with a calm,
> slightly nasal resonance and a smooth, lightly gravelly texture. Unhurried
> authority: flat, deadpan delivery with deliberate pauses, never excited by the
> catastrophe it describes. Close, intimate studio recording, dry and clean with no
> reverb, no music bleed and no processing artefacts. Audio voiceover only, strictly
> no speech bubbles, no dialogue boxes.

Style 4 rule applied: the narrator is identical in all three clips — the scene change
never recasts him. Emotion lives in the pictures and the score, not in his register.

## The frozen character (repeat verbatim wherever he appears)

Style 4 default protagonist anchor:

> One minimalist 2D cartoon protagonist with a white filled circular head, two small
> dark oval eyes, simple eyebrow strokes and a short curved mouth. The torso is a slim
> closed tapered shape with solid white fill inside its dark outline. Both arms and both
> legs are dark single-line strokes with constant medium weight; hands are small dark
> rounded shapes and feet are short dark rounded strokes. No hair or clothing
> accessories. Keep the same head-to-body proportions, white head and torso fill, and
> outline weight in every shot. Exactly two arms and two legs exist; a moving limb
> replaces its previous pose rather than leaving another limb behind.

Recurring cast: **the homeowner** (above) and **the mosquito** — one tiny black
mosquito with a dark needle proboscis and blurred vibrating wings. The mosquito is
never harmed, never changes size within a shot, and is never joined by a second insect.

## Clip 1 — `00:00–00:08` · The Hook and the Self-Slap

**Beat 1 `0–3 s`** — Night. The homeowner lies asleep on his back under the duvet, head
on the pillow, eyes drawn as two closed lines, small sleep puffs drifting up. A tiny
black mosquito buzzes in from the top-right of the vertical frame in a wandering,
looping path, gets loud, lands on the tip of his nose and settles. His eyes snap open
as two glowing vivid-red ovals, eyebrows slamming into an angry V, two manga-style
detached anger crosses flashing beside his head. Camera pushes in a slow vertical
creep toward the pillow and the frame tints faint warm red.

**Beat 2 `3–5.5 s`** — He throws the duvet aside and stands beside the bed, furious. He
winds up and delivers an enormous slap across his own face. MISS: the mosquito flicks
away, the slap lands on his own cheek, leaving one small vivid-red hand mark. Impact
stars and short radial speed lines burst from the cheek, three small dizzy stars orbit
above his head, his mouth becomes a small dark oval.

**Beat 3 `5.5–8 s`** — The mosquito loops back and hovers tauntingly. He reaches down
and yanks out a giant flyswatter — a wooden handle with a huge vivid-red perforated
paddle the size of a surfboard — from beside the bed. He swings it in a wide vertical
arc across the frame, torso twisting, then freezes, shocked: the mosquito is perched
on the middle of the paddle, unharmed, wings still blurred.

Cut on the frozen swing.

---

## Clip 2 — `00:08–00:17` · The Escalation and the Arsenal

**Beat 1 `0–3 s`** — Continued from the swing. The swatter smashes down and wipes out
the bedroom in three hits: the dresser folds into planks and the warm glass lamp
crashes in a burst of amber shards; the oval mirror shatters into a spider web of
cracks and slumps off the wall; the little stool and the potted plant tumble. The bed
itself collapses into rubble, the pale blue duvet flopping over the debris, feathers
and dust hanging in the air. The mosquito darts between the blows in tiny
kung-fu flourishes, unharmed and smug; each miss raises a plume of dust.

**Beat 2 `3–6 s`** — Breathing hard with small sweat drops flicking off his head, he
hauls a wooden weapons crate into the frame and kicks it open. He pulls out a long
olive bazooka, rests it on his shoulder, and fires straight down the vertical frame:
a bright muzzle flash, the recoil slamming him back, a skid mark on the floor. Nothing
dies. The mosquito flies happily through the smoke. He flings the bazooka away.

**Beat 3 `6–9 s`** — He spins up a red-tanked flamethrower and sprays a wall of vivid
red and golden-orange fire that fills the lower half of the frame, scorching the walls
and starting small fires around the wrecked room. In the middle of the flames, the
mosquito hovers completely unbothered in a small clear bubble of untouched air.

---

## Clip 3 — `00:17–00:25` · The Orbital Strike and the Punchline

**Beat 1 `0–3 s`** — Losing his mind, he slams his fist on an emergency console that
rises out of the crate: a hinged metal safety cover flips open, one big vivid-red dome
button rises, and he presses it. A thin warm-gold target line stabs down from the very
top of the vertical frame and locks onto him with a pulsing ring on the floor. He looks
up, eyes wide, mouth open. The line thickens into a giant crimson orbital laser beam
that slams down the full height of the frame, hitting the ground in a blinding white
flash that whites out the screen.

**Beat 2 `3–5 s`** — White fades into the aftermath. The house is gone: a baked-earth
crater scene under a dark smoke sky, ash and drifting dust, a deep crater with glowing
molten cracks, chunky rubble slabs and snapped beams ringed around it. The homeowner
stands in the smoking crater, singed, one small sweat drop on his head, hands on his
hips, chest rising and falling with heavy proud breaths, a proud closed-eye smile —
the posture of a man who has won. Slow triumphant push-in.

**Beat 3 `5–8 s`** — The tiny mosquito flies back into frame and toward the camera
until it is huge on the lens: glossy black body, blurred wings, a dark halo around its
silhouette, completely unharmed. Reaction cut: his eyes become two white circles with
tiny pupils, three shock lines above his head. He faints straight backwards, landing
flat in the dust with a puff, eyes drawn as two small x marks. The camera holds on the
idiot insect on the lens as the picture fades to black.

## How this cut was built locally

```
shots.py       the four-shot timeline (poses, cameras, effects, cut points)
sticklib.py    Style 4 character rig, camera, vector drawing, particles, glow
props.py       bedroom, progressive destruction states, weapons, emergency console, crater
fx.py          mosquito, dust/debris banks, fire, laser, blast, comic marks
audio.py       full 25 s soundtrack (score + SFX + narrator placement) -> audio/mix.wav
render.py      streams 750 frames into ffmpeg -> out/mosquito-vs-stickman.mp4
```

`python3 render.py` reproduces the film; `python3 audio.py && python3 render.py`
reproduces picture and sound. The narrator lines live in `audio/vo*.mp3`.
